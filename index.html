See how the weather will affect your site before it strikes.

We're a small, U.S.-based engineering company. We take high-resolution weather, fluid simulation and low-cost sensors and turn them into digital twins, so launch sites, shipping teams and defense programs can plan their day around what is actually about to happen.

What we actually do

Our work falls into six areas. They have one thing in common: we connect the physics to the people who have to make a call, whether that is a launch director, a logistics manager or a program lead.

  • Digital thread

    One traceable record that runs from the first CAD model to the hardware on the pad, so anyone can ask where a number came from.

    Read more

  • Real-time CFD

    Fluid simulation on GPUs, fast enough to use while the decision is still open.

    Read more

  • LuminaBox tracking

    Small sensors that ride on containers, tankers and vehicles and report where they are and what they have been through.

    Read more

  • Counter-UAS

    Putting several sensors together into one picture of what is in the air, and accounting for how wind and nearby buildings affect it.

    Read more

  • Weather digital twin

    Forecasts refined down to the scale of a building and shown on a 3D model of the site.

    Try it

  • Consulting

    Engineers who embed with defense and space teams and work on their problem alongside them.

    Read more
