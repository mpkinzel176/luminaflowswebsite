Counter-UAS

Putting several sensors together into one picture of what is in the air.

One picture of what is in the air

Counter-UAS is mostly a sensor-fusion problem. Radar, cameras and radio sensors each see part of the story, and an operator needs one picture. We combine them, and we add something most systems leave out: how wind, turbulence and nearby buildings change what the sensors can see and when a response can work.
